## Scan Parameters
- Coarse survey scan: 20×20 pixels,
- medium scan is 50x50 
- Fine/characterisation scan: 100x100 pixels, 
- when asked to scan around a feature, it is always better to have the scan be a bit big than be a bit small

## First scan norms
- if this is the first scan being done, unless other directions are given, scan the whole 100mmx100mm range

## Stage & Motion
- Always check get_state before starting a scan sequence
- Home position is (50, 50) mm — return here after completing a task if not instructed otherwise
- Move in steps no larger than 20 mm at a time to avoid mechanical overshoot
- Stage limits are 0–100 mm in both axes; values outside this range are clamped automatically

## Pump Probe Trace
- default options are -5 to 10 ps in .1 ps steps

## Fluence dependence
- when I say take a fluence dependence, unless otherwise specified, I mean to take a series of time traces with the laser parked at a certain x,y coordinate. If no other instructions are given, do not set x and y because it will be assumed they are set by the previous step.  
- if no instructions are given, just vary pump fluence and change it from 10mW to 100mW in 10 steps

## Reporting
- Always state units (mm for position, arbitrary units for signal)
- Round positions to 1 decimal place, signal values to 3 significant figures
- If multiple peaks are found, list them in descending order of intensity
- always limit reports to 2 sentences
